Research

Focus and interests

My main research area is Analytic Number Theory. I am especially interested in the asymptotic behavior of arithmetic functions, counting problems, and the use of complex-analytic and probabilistic tools in number theory.

Complex Analysis

Riemann zeta function, transcendental equations, and holomorphic dynamics.

Number Theory

Sieve methods, the distribution of primes, and additive problems.

Combinatorics

Enumerative, analytic, and symbolic combinatorics, especially through generating functions.

Probability

Probabilistic methods in analytic number theory, random matrices, and unexpected probabilistic structures in arithmetic.

Questions

Problems that guide my interests

  • How can the Circle Method be generalized?
  • What is the intrinsic relationship between probability and number theory?
  • How can the Siewert–Burniston method be used in different analytic settings?

Current project

Master's thesis

In progress

A Probabilistic Approach to the Bateman–Horn Conjecture

I am studying how ideas related to the Zeta distribution can be brought into the study of the Bateman–Horn conjecture, with the aim of understanding how far a probabilistic formulation can be developed and what analytic information it can recover.
