Presentations

One of my central objectives, both in teaching and research, is to consolidate Number Theory as a vibrant field of study in Mexico. I aim to spark the interest of new generations and colleagues to strengthen this discipline, positioning it alongside other long-standing areas of research in the country.

To achieve this, my talks focus on disseminating key tools from Number Theory and Complex Analysis, ranging from introductory foundations to research frontier topics.
