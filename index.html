Graduate student in Mathematics · UNAM

Analytic Number Theory, Complex Analysis, teaching, and mathematical outreach.

Hi! I'm Lorenzo, a mathematician from the School of Sciences at UNAM and currently a graduate student at the Institute of Mathematics. My research lies mainly in Analytic Number Theory, with a particular interest in the interaction between analytic and probabilistic methods.

I also teach at the School of Sciences and participate actively in mathematical outreach through the Festival Matemático and the Seminario de Becarios.
